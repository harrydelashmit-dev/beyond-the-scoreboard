## Reproducible content and research safeguards

- `config/presentation_deck.json` stores the slide content, speaker assignments, timing and notes.
- `docs/speaker_notes.md` is a readable copy of the embedded notes.
- `python -m scoreboard.presentation_notes` rebuilds these two content files from the approved outline and Python configuration. It does not rerun financial models or overwrite the saved presentation.
- All source analysis and numerical chart generation remain Python. Native slide formatting uses the presentation editor.
- 94% is GAO's statistic for whole Division I athletics departments in 2023–2024, separate from our 2010–2019 panel. It is not a football-only deficit statistic.
- The deck presents associations and uncertain findings, not causal ROI. The accounting revenue measure excludes allocated support but retains transfer/definition limitations.
- Cost recovery is tested against dollar-for-dollar coverage. The backup slide discloses the simpler school/year level-model interval that includes full coverage.
- Recruitment findings do not disprove the Flutie Effect. Sparse negative AP-event comparisons are disclosed as exploratory evidence.
