# Five-member presentation plan (20 minutes)

This is a working Track A outline, not a finished slide deck. Replace role labels with the five actual group members. Each member must make substantive commits from their own GitHub account and be able to explain the entire project.

| Member | Time | Content and repository contribution |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–3:30 | Decision question, executive summary, main findings; own proposal and framing |
| 2 | 3:30–7:00 | Sources, school-year joins, missing observations and coverage; own data audit |
| 3 | 7:00–11:00 | Spending/performance charts and school/year comparison; own interpretation and validation |
| 4 | 11:00–15:30 | Applications/enrollment and football/basketball comparisons; own robustness review |
| 5 | 15:30–20:00 | Generated-revenue distinction, break-even scenarios, limits and administrative implications; own final synthesis |

Suggested slides: title with five names; executive findings; why this allocation decision matters; data chain and timeline; sample coverage; spending versus performance; within-university results; applications and enrollment; sport visibility comparison if validated; accounting support versus generated revenue; break-even contribution chart; limitations and next decisions; references and backup regression definitions.

Use generated charts at full size. Explain that a confidence interval crossing zero means the sample does not clearly distinguish positive and negative associations under the model. Do not describe insignificance as proof of no effect. Present exact sample sizes and avoid using all-DI counts for a smaller regression sample.

Before final slides: have someone outside the group interpret the charts, record confusion, fix it, rehearse to 18–22 minutes, consult the instructor, and determine the assigned December 7 or December 9 slot. Commit the final slides in this repository. Instructor sharing and group evaluations remain human actions.
